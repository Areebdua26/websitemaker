About LensFlair

LensFlair is a photography blog dedicated to helping photographers of all levels improve their craft. Founded in 2015 by professional photographer Sarah Johnson, our mission is to make photography accessible and enjoyable for everyone.

We provide in-depth tutorials, gear reviews, post-processing guides, and inspiration to help you capture stunning images and develop your unique photographic style.

Whether you're just starting out or you're a seasoned pro, you'll find valuable resources and a supportive community here at LensFlair.
